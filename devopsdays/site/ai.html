Enterprise AI, embedded in the platform

We didn't build another chatbot next to the platform. We embedded AI inside the workflows the platform already governs — where the incidents, the documents and the source code already live. Three services, one boundary.

01
Knowledge Assistant

Grounded in the organisation's own documentation. It knows who you are, answers within your scope, and cites the exact documents behind every answer.
02
Architecture Discovery and Analysis

Reads the repositories and configuration the platform governs, reconstructs the real architecture, and surfaces the gaps nobody documented.
03
Pipeline Incident Analysis

A deployment fails; the platform collects logs, events, config diffs and release history — and the on-prem model explains the root cause with recommended actions.
